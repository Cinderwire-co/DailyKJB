# Daily KJB privacy policy

Effective November 25, 2026

Daily KJB does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs that track you.

## Your reading, notes and highlights

Your name (if you give one), reading plan, progress, notes, highlights and settings are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are included in your device backups as with any app. Deleting the app deletes everything.

## Apple Intelligence

On devices that have it, Daily KJB uses Apple's on-device language model to write study notes on a verse, explain a word in plain English, and write a quiz on the day's reading. The verse text and everything else it works from are processed on the device and are not sent to Daily KJB or any server.

## Focus Mode and Screen Time

Focus Mode uses Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) to keep the apps you choose closed until you've read. iOS holds your choices as private tokens that Daily KJB cannot read: the app never learns which apps you chose or how you use them. An accountability passcode, if you set one, is stored on the device only as a one-way hash.

## Notifications

Reading reminders are scheduled on the device. No notification server is involved.

## Children

Daily KJB does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
