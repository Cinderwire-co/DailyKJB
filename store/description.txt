Daily KJB helps you read the King James Bible every day, and understand what you read.

A PLAN FOR EVERY READER
• The whole Bible in order, or as events happened (chronological)
• The Old Testament, or the New Testament a passage a day
• Over 3 months, 6 months, a year or two years; the chapters are shared out evenly
• Add a Psalm or a Proverb each day alongside
• Fall behind? Pick up from today, and nothing you've read is lost

A BIBLE YOU TURN LIKE A BOOK
• Swipe from chapter to chapter, with a gentle tap of haptics as each page turns
• A verse to a line, or as a paragraph; Garamond, Georgia or a clean type, at the size you like
• Tap verses to highlight in five colours, write a note, copy, or share
• Search every word of the Bible, or go straight to a reference

STUDY EVERY WORD, OFFLINE
• Tap any word to see the Hebrew or Greek behind it, from Strong's Exhaustive Concordance
• See what the word meant in 1828, from Noah Webster's American Dictionary
• Cross references for every verse
• All of it built into the app: no connection needed

STUDY HELP FROM APPLE INTELLIGENCE
On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Daily KJB can write short study notes on a verse, explain a word in plain English from Strong's and Webster's together, and write a quiz on the day's reading. It all happens on your iPhone, privately, with no connection. On other devices, every study tool above still works.

FOCUS MODE: THE WORD BEFORE THE WORLD
Choose the apps that pull you away. Each day they stay closed until you've read; mark the day read and they open at once. An accountability partner can set a passcode so Focus Mode can't be turned off on a whim. Uses Apple's Screen Time, which keeps your choices private: Daily KJB never sees which apps you choose.

AND MORE
• A verse for each day, to read, study, or share as a picture for a post or a story
• Your notes and highlights in one journal, searchable
• Progress: days read, days in a row, and every day of the plan on its calendar
• A quiz after each day's reading; pass it and the day is marked read
• Parchment by day, lamplight by night
• Gentle reminders at the time you choose

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your reading, notes and highlights stay on your phone.

Apple Intelligence features need an iPhone 15 Pro or later (or an iPad with Apple Intelligence) running iOS 26 or later, with Apple Intelligence turned on.
